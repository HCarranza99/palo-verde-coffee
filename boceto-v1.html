01 — THE FARM

Picked by hand, one cherry at a time.

At 1,600 m on our family farm, only fully ripe red cherries make the cut. Tito checks every branch twice.

02 — THE ROAST

Small batches, roasted the slow way.

12-minute profiles in a 15 kg drum. We pull it at City+ so the fruit stays bright and the sweetness stays put.

03 — THE BAG

Sealed within hours of roasting.

Nitrogen-flushed bags with a one-way valve. Roast date on every bag — never a "best by" guess.

04 — THE TRIP

Straight from the farm. No middlemen.

Direct trade means the farmer earns up to 3× the commodity price — and your coffee skips six months in a warehouse.

05 — YOUR DOOR

Fresh at your door in 3 days.

Free shipping anywhere in the US on orders over $40. Tito says hi.
